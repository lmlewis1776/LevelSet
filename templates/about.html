About L. M. Lewis Consulting

LevelSet is powered by L. M. Lewis Consulting — a Los Angeles-based firm specializing in organizational effectiveness, technology strategy, and capacity building.

Our Story

LevelSet was born from years of hands-on consulting experience. We saw the same challenge over and over: small to mid-size organizations desperately needed the kind of organizational health assessments and strategic guidance that only expensive consultants could provide. The organizations serving our most vulnerable communities were being priced out of the tools they needed to grow stronger.

So we built LevelSet — packaging decades of consulting expertise into an affordable, self-serve SaaS platform. Same quality insights. Fraction of the cost.

Our Approach

We believe every organization deserves access to professional-grade assessment tools, regardless of budget. Our tools are built on proven frameworks, designed with cultural responsiveness, and delivered through a modern, accessible platform.

Our Expertise

With 15+ years of experience working with nonprofits, startups, and community-based organizations, L. M. Lewis Consulting brings deep expertise in:

  • Organizational development and capacity building
  • Technology strategy and digital transformation
  • Workforce effectiveness measurement
  • Strategic planning for mission-driven organizations
