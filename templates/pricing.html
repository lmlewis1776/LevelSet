Frequently Asked Questions

What do I get with the free plan?

You get one free self-assessment report of your choice. After that, you can purchase individual reports for $49 each or subscribe for unlimited access.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade from Free to Subscription at any time, or cancel your subscription and move back to Free.

How are the assessments scored?

Each assessment uses a weighted scoring system across multiple dimensions. Your results include an overall score, dimension-by-dimension breakdown, and actionable recommendations.

Who is behind LevelSet?

LevelSet is powered by L. M. Lewis Consulting, bringing 15+ years of DEI and nonprofit consulting expertise into an affordable SaaS platform.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All assessment data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your organization's data with third parties.
