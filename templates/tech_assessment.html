BC/DR Assessment

Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (BC/DR) Assessment

Evaluate your organization's resilience against sudden power loss, critical hardware failures, and malicious cyber threats. All 16 questions are strictly required to generate your security analysis.

Infrastructure Redundancy & Power

1. Do you utilize a dedicated staging/sandbox environment to thoroughly test all software and OS upgrades before pushing them live to production?

2. Do you have a secondary, automated 'failover' internet connection from a completely different service provider (e.g., fiber + cellular) to protect your operations?

3. Do you have a professionally installed Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) for all critical local network hardware, and is it tested quarterly under a live load?

4. If your primary corporate workspace becomes physically inaccessible tomorrow, is a pre-configured remote-work infrastructure ready for your entire team?

Data Protection & Secure Backups

5. Do you explicitly follow the 3-2-1 backup strategy (3 copies of data, across 2 different media types, with at least 1 copy isolated completely offsite)?

6. Are your critical organizational backups 'immutable' (cannot be altered) or completely 'air-gapped' to protect them from encryption during a ransomware attack?

7. Have you calculated and documented your exact Recovery Point Objective (RPO)—defining how many hours or days of data your organization can afford to lose?

8. When was the last time your team performed a live 'Fire Drill' data restoration to prove that your backup archives are readable and can be fully integrated?

Crisis Communications & Digital Presence

9. Do you have a secondary, secure out-of-band communication channel (e.g., Signal or independent Slack) that remains functional if primary business email goes dark?

10. Is there an official, written Crisis Communication Plan that explicitly identifies who is authorized to speak to stakeholders, staff, and the public during an active emergency?

11. Do you maintain an emergency contact directory for all staff, key vendors, and primary clients that is fully accessible outside of your main corporate server network?

12. Do you have pre-drafted communication alert templates (for social media, client notifications, and email blasts) ready to deploy instantly during a digital disruption?

Operational Recovery & Resilience

13. Do you have a designated, cross-functional Disaster Recovery Team consisting of representatives from IT, Help Desk, Executive Leadership, and Customer Support?

14. Do you have a comprehensive, step-by-step Business Continuity Plan (BCP) manual detailing how to maintain manual or minimal business operations during a systemic outage?

15. Have you explicitly calculated your Recovery Time Objective (RTO)—the maximum threshold of time your organization can stay offline before facing collapse?

16. Does your leadership and crisis response team conduct at least one comprehensive annual simulation (Tabletop Exercise) to walk through a real-world disaster scenario?
